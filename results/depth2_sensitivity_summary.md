# Depth-2 fix sensitivity to served-load threshold (case30, 10 audited INFEASIBLE rungs)

- **0.99 (paper's threshold):** 0/10 have a verified 2-action fix — exactly reproduces `depth2_v2.log` (same 10 scenarios, same pair order, same "none after 500 pairs" on every row).
- **0.95:** 2/10 fixed. Line 14 @ scale 1.3094 -> `raise generator 4 voltage setpoint by 0.02 pu` + `shed 25% of load 4` (serves 96.0% of pre-action load). Line 10 @ scale 1.2895 -> `raise generator 0 voltage setpoint by 0.02 pu` + `shed 25% of load 4` (serves 96.0%).
- **0.90:** still only 2/10 fixed — no additional scenarios unlock relative to 0.95. Line 10 @ 1.2895 keeps the identical pair/served-fraction as at 0.95 (96.0%, already above 0.90). Line 14 @ 1.3094 switches to an earlier pair in the fixed search order, `shed 25% of load 3` + `shed 25% of load 4` (serves 93.0%), which clears violations but only meets the looser 0.90 bar, not 0.95.
- **Which scenarios ever get fixed:** only the two least-severe rungs — the first rung past each line's infeasibility boundary (line 14 @ 1.3094, line 10 @ 1.2895). None of the other 8 rungs (line 14 @ 1.3356/1.3748/1.4403/1.5713/1.7677; line 10 @ 1.3152/1.3539/1.4184) find a 2-action fix at any of the three thresholds within the 500-pair budget.
- **Takeaway:** the 0/10 result is robust between 0.99 and 0.95 relaxation only unlocks 2 of 10 scenarios, and relaxing further to 0.90 buys nothing more (still 2/10). The finding is not a knife's-edge artifact of the 99% cutoff; it reflects that most rungs are simply deeper than depth-2 can reach, while the two boundary-adjacent rungs are recoverable once >=95% load service is treated as an acceptable "fix."
